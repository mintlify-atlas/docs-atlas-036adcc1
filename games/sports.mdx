The sports section of CXSHS Game Hub covers a wide field: American football management, street basketball, international soccer, billiards, fishing, and motorsport racing all live here as fully offline HTML files. Whether you want a quick two-minute game of 1-on-1 Soccer or a deep Retro Bowl franchise season, every title launches instantly from the `offline/` directory with no assets to download and no account to create.

<Note>
  Most multiplayer sports titles (Basketball Legends, Football Legends, Soccer Bros, etc.) support **same-keyboard two-player** mode — one player uses WASD and the other uses arrow keys, so no extra hardware is needed.
</Note>

<Tip>
  **Retro Bowl** saves its entire franchise state automatically in your browser's `localStorage`. To back up a long season, use the Hub's built-in **Notes** app to record key stats or export save data before clearing browser storage.
</Tip>
