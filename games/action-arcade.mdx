The action and arcade category is the largest in the CXSHS Game Hub, covering everything from tight precision platformers and rhythm battles to survival shooters and motorcycle stunts. Every title is a single self-contained HTML file in the `offline/` folder — no loading screens, no installs, no network required. Whether you have two minutes for a quick Flappy Bird run or two hours to dive deep into Five Nights at Freddy's, this section has you covered.
